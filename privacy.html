Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Idea Stage Funding Hub

What this site is

Idea Stage Funding Hub is a free research directory of idea-stage funding programs. It lists accelerators, grants, pre-seed funds, fellowships and pitch competitions, with deadlines, eligibility catches and links to each program's official application page. There is no account, login or signup on this site, and the site itself does not ask visitors for a name, email address or any other personal details.

Information we collect

Directly, we collect nothing: there are no forms, bookings, subscriptions or comments on this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address and browser type) as part of hosting the site. Program listings are compiled from public program websites and secondary sources; no visitor data is sold or shared by us.

Cookies and third-party advertising

This site shows advertisements served by Adsterra. Adsterra and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads and measure their performance. This site may also show advertisements served by Google AdSense in the future. When AdSense ads are live, Google and its partners may use cookies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time via Google Ads Settings, and you can read how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; blocking cookies may affect how ads display, but the directory itself will still work.

Analytics

No third-party analytics tracking script is running on this site at present. Google Search Console is used only to check whether the site is indexed by Google Search; it does not track individual visitors on this site.

External links

Links on this site point to third-party program websites, including official application pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and you should read their privacy policies before submitting any application.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.
