About this directory

Idea Stage Funding Hub is a free research directory of funding programs that back founders at the idea stage — before product, revenue or incorporation. It covers accelerators, equity-free grants, pre-seed funds, fellowships, company builders and pitch competitions from every region of the world, India included.

How programs are verified

Every listing carries a badge. “Official site” means the details were read on the program's own website. “Second-hand” means they come from news, aggregators or blogs, and should be treated as a lead to verify on the official page before relying on them. A low-verification badge means the sourcing was thin — amounts, dates, and sometimes even whether the program still runs, need re-checking.

Where a deadline or intake could not be confirmed at all, the listing says so plainly instead of guessing. Programs that require revenue, a launched product, an MVP, local residency in another region, or student status are listed under “Ruled out” with the reason on the card, so they are useful again once your stage changes. Closed programs stay on as a dated historical archive.

Who it was built for

The directory was built for a solo technical founder, based in India, with no registered company, no revenue and no users — building an AI business-management SaaS and an AI adaptive-learning platform. That is why idea-stage eligibility is the filter: if a program needs traction first, it does not belong in the main list.

How to use it

  • Filter by status: open now, watch list, ruled out, or closed.
  • Read the “catch” on each card first — relocation, in-person residencies and team requirements rule out more founders than idea quality does.
  • Re-check the deadline on the official page — windows close early when cohorts fill.
  • Prefer a warm intro where a fund has no public application portal.
  • Never pay an application fee to any program or intermediary — see the scams to avoid page.

Disclaimer

This site is an independent directory and is not affiliated with any program listed. Amounts, deadlines and eligibility change without notice — the official application page is the final word.
